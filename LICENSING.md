The games are ports of the web version in [insert-coin](https://github.com/DarkHelmet67/insert-coin) (MIT, same author). Space Invaders and Breakout are rebuilt from research on the original hardware and software, with no ROM.

**Galaga is different.** Its aliens fly programs stored in the original ROM and its music is a program for Namco's sound chip, so the web version, and this port after it, carry data tables taken from the original 1981 ROMs and PROMs: `games/galaga/src/rom.c` (game tables, flight programs, sound data, waveform and colour PROMs) and `games/galaga/src/art.c` (sprites and characters), generated by `tools/galaga/extract-rom.mjs`. That data is Namco's work, not ours: the GPL covers our code around it, not the data itself. It is here for the study and faithful preservation of a 45-year-old game, in a non-commercial hobby project; if a rights holder objects, the Galaga cartridge will be removed.
